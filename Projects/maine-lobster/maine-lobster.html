How to eat the whole thing

First time ordering the whole dinner? Here's the order of operations. Take your time - the tide it's going anywhere.

1
Claws first

Twist them off and crack them open. The knuckle meat next door is the sweetest bite in the whole lobster.

2
Take the tail.

Twist is away from the body and snap off the little flippers at the end

3
Push, don't dig.

Push the tail meat out i one piece with your fork. Thumb also works. Nobody's judging.

4
Don't skip the legs.

Bite down gently and squeeze the meat out like a straw. This is where the locals live.
